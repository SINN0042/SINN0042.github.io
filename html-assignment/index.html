About me

Hi there! My passions are surfing, snowboarding and guitar. I like to dabble in photography and other instruments from time to time. I am originally from Montreal, although I’ve lived in Australia for a little while. The coast of Victoria is where I learned most of my surfing. My dad and I continue with amazing surf trips twice a year.

Most recently, one of thoses trips my girlfriend of one year came to Australia to meet my family and I got to try to teach her my hobby. Together, we rented a car and explored the City of Melbourne and all along the Great Ocean Road. We saw wild kangaroos, penguins and a koala. This trip gave us the travel bug, we are now talking about our next adventure.
